Questions owners ask.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your place: rooms, tables, staff and the support you want. Contact us and we send you a clear quote after a short call.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. Switch the till to offline and keep taking orders and payments. Everything saves on the device and syncs when the connection returns.

Which printers can I use?

Any thermal receipt printer your browser can print to, in 58 mm or 80 mm. Start the browser in kiosk printing mode and tickets print without asking each time.

Which devices does it run on?

Any tablet, phone or computer with a modern browser. There is nothing to install, and it fills the screen like an app.

Can I use my own currency, VAT and language on receipts?

Yes. Set your currency label, your VAT percentage and your thank-you message in settings. They appear on every ticket.

How do I control what staff can do?

Each person signs in with their own PIN and a role: owner, manager, waiter or kitchen. You can tick or untick each permission per person.

Where is my data stored?

Your menu, tables, users and sales are saved on your device, and you can export a full backup file whenever you like.
